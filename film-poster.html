PROJECT 07 / PRINT & VISUAL STORYTELLING

Film Poster Design

A story told through image, mood, and type.

This project showcases an original film poster concept built around cinematic composition and visual storytelling. A double-exposure portrait combines a human profile with a landscape and solitary figures, creating a visual metaphor for memory, distance, and inner reflection.

The restrained monochrome palette, subtle warm tones, birds in motion, and expressive Arabic title work together to establish atmosphere and guide the viewer’s attention. Every element is arranged to communicate the film’s mood before the story is even read.

The design focuses on balancing imagery and typography, creating depth through layered photographs, and maintaining clear hierarchy across the poster.

DESIGN NOTES

Visual Storytelling

The double-exposure treatment connects the character’s inner world with the surrounding landscape. High-contrast black and white keeps the portrait striking, while the Arabic lettering gives the composition a distinctive cultural and editorial voice.
