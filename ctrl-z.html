CTRL+Z

REWIND YOUR IDEAS.

CTRL+Z is our graduation project from the Graphic Design Diploma at Creativa, in collaboration with the Ministry of Communications and Information Technology (MCIT) and the National Telecommunication Institute (NTI).

Inspired by the culture of the early 2000s and reimagined for 2026, CTRL+Z is an entertainment hub that brings together watching, listening, capturing moments, and gaming. It blends nostalgic references with contemporary visual design to create an immersive experience where the past meets the present.

Throughout this journey, I had the opportunity to apply the skills I developed during the diploma, explore creative ideas, and collaborate with my team to bring the concept to life—from the first brainstorming sessions to the final outcome.
